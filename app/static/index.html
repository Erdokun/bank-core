A few things to know

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What can I do with Veyra?

Manage four currency balances, transfer within the Veyra ledger, create savings spaces, review activity and customize your workspace. The demo lets you try these flows with sample money.

How does the demo work?

The demo runs in your browser with sample balances. No real money moves, and no bank account or payment card is issued. Demo changes are saved on this device.

How do I create an account?

Choose Get started, enter your details and verify your email with a six-digit code. Email delivery must be configured on the running service. Real accounts begin with zero balances.

Can I make it feel like my own?

Yes. Choose light or dark mode inside your workspace, set your preferred currency, personalize your profile and adjust your privacy and security preferences.
